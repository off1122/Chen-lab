# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-glasses" %}Postdoctoral Fellow

We are looking for postdoctoral fellows in bioinformatics, molecular biology, or related fields. Candidates should have a PhD, first‑author SCI publications, and strong skills in either wet‑lab or dry‑lab research. Responsibilities include leading independent projects, mentoring students, and contributing to grant writing. 

For consideration, please send your CV and a brief cover letter to chenchangya@ihcams.ac.cn

# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-graduation-cap" %}Graduate students

Graduate students in our lab are recruited through the Institute of Hematology, Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences.
Our students have access to shared research facilities and expertise across hematology, genomics, and computational biology. We provide close mentorship and an interdisciplinary research environment, while encouraging students to develop their own scientific questions and pursue independent research. Our goal is to help trainees build strong experimental and computational skills and prepare them for careers in biomedical research.
