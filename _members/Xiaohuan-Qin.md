Xiaohuan received her bachelor’s degree in Clinical Medicine from Southern Medical University in 2021. 
She subsequently  pursued graduate training at the Institute of Hematology, Peking Union Medical College, to specialize in computational hematology. Her research focuses on epigenetic regulation and therapy resistance in leukemia. 
Outside the lab, Xiaohuan enjoys playing badminton, watching documentaries, and exploring new landscapes through travel.
