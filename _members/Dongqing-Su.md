Dongqing Su received her Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from Harbin Medical University. 
Her research focuses on bioinformatics and single-cell omics, with a particular interest in developing machine learning and deep learning-based computational methods to investigate disease mechanisms and uncover complex biological processes.
Outside of research, she enjoys exploring new cuisines and swimming.
