Ziying Yang obtained her Ph.D. in Genomics from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences.
Her research focuses on the mechanisms underlying drug resistance in leukemic stem cells, with a particular interest in the interactions between leukemia cells and the bone marrow microenvironment. 
Outside of research, she enjoys traveling.
